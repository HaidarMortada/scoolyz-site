Delete your Scoolyz account

You can delete your account yourself in the app, or ask us here if you no longer have the app or can't sign in.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Scoolyz and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture at the top, then Profile.
  3. Open Delete my account, read what will happen, enter your password and confirm.

Your account is closed at once: you are signed out everywhere and can't sign in again.

Students: the app asks your parents first. Your account closes when one of them confirms on their profile; until then you can cancel the request.

What is deleted

Your account, which is the part that belongs to you:

  • your sign-in (user name or email address and password), which can no longer be used;
  • your profile photo;
  • the devices signed in to your account, and their notification tokens;
  • your notifications inside Scoolyz and your app settings;
  • your Ask Scoolyz conversations.

What your school keeps

Scoolyz is run for your school, and the school is responsible for its school records. Deleting your account does not delete them, because schools must keep many of them (often by law):

  • student files, enrolment, attendance, marks and report cards;
  • fees, payments and receipts;
  • health records kept by the school clinic;
  • notices, homework and messages exchanged within the school, which keep the sender's name.

If you want any of these erased too, say so in your request (or ask your school directly). Your school decides what it may erase and tells you if a record must be kept, and for how long.

Deleting a parent's account does not delete the children's own student sign-ins. Tick the box below if you want those closed as well.
