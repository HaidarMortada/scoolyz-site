Terms of use

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · DRAFT for review

These terms are the rules for using Scoolyz: the Scoolyz app (Android, Windows), each school's site at <school>.scoolyz.com, and scoolyz.com. By signing in you agree to them. If you are a child, please read them with a parent.

1. Who provides Scoolyz

Scoolyz is provided by Haidar Mortada, in Lebanon ("Scoolyz", "we"). We make the software and run it for the schools that use it. Contact: haidarmortada30@gmail.com.

Your school is the one that gives you your account. A school that uses Scoolyz may also have its own agreement with us; between the school and us, that agreement comes first.

2. Your school controls its data

  • The records in your school's Scoolyz (students, families, marks, attendance, fees, health, notices and messages) belong to the school. The school decides what is recorded, who can see it and how long it is kept.
  • We store and process these records only to run Scoolyz for the school, as our privacy policy explains. We do not sell them, show advertising or use them for anything else.
  • Marks, fees, the timetable and other school information are entered by the school. If something looks wrong, ask your school: it is the one that can correct it, and its decisions are its own.

3. Your account

  • Your school creates your account. Nobody can sign up in the app on their own.
  • An account is for one person. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what is done with your account. A parent may help a young child use theirs.
  • If you think someone else has used your account, change your password and tell your school.
  • Your school can change, pause or close the accounts it created.

4. Acceptable use

Use Scoolyz only for school life: following and running your school. Do not:

  • break the law, or use Scoolyz to help anyone else break it;
  • try to see records you are not meant to see, sign in as someone else, or reach another school's Scoolyz;
  • get around Scoolyz's security, test it for weaknesses, or attack, overload or disrupt it;
  • copy data out of Scoolyz with automated tools, or copy, change or take apart the app, except where the law allows it;
  • upload viruses or other harmful files;
  • use Scoolyz to advertise, sell or send spam.

5. Posts and messages

These rules apply to everything people write in Scoolyz: comments on notices, class posts and replies, homework comments, live-lesson chat, messages, and files and photos you send. Your school may add rules of its own.

Be kind and respectful. Write as you would in the classroom. Do not post or send:

  • bullying, harassment, insults or threats;
  • hate against anyone for their religion, sect, ethnicity, nationality, sex, disability or any similar reason;
  • sexual content of any kind. Anything that sexualises a child is reported to the authorities;
  • violence, or anything that encourages self-harm, or the use of weapons, drugs or alcohol;
  • personal information, yours or anyone else's: phone numbers, addresses, email addresses or social media accounts. A student's message with these waits for a teacher's approval;
  • photos or recordings of people without their permission, including screenshots of other people's messages or live lessons shared outside the school;
  • pretending to be someone else, or to speak for the school when you don't;
  • false information meant to mislead, spam, advertising or chain messages;
  • material you don't have the right to share, or anything else that is illegal.

What you write stays yours. You allow the school and Scoolyz to store it and show it to the people it was meant for (your class, the notice's readers, the staff), only to run Scoolyz. Staff can see what is written in school spaces, and the school decides what is kept.

6. Reports, blocking and paused posting

  • Report. Every comment, post, chat message and person has a Report button. Choose a reason and, if you like, add a note. The report goes to your school; the person is not told who reported them.
  • Who decides. The school's moderators (staff the school chooses, such as the principal) review reports as soon as they can. They can:
    • dismiss the report if nothing is wrong;
    • remove the content, which is hidden for everyone and kept for staff to review;
    • warn the author;
    • pause posting for 7 days, 30 days or until the school lifts it.
    Moderators can also remove content they see themselves, without a report. Every decision is logged with who made it, when and why.
  • You are told. If something you wrote is removed, you are warned, or your posting is paused, the app tells you, and it tells you again when you can post again.
  • Paused posting. While it lasts you can't comment, post or chat. You can still read notices, homework and marks and use the rest of Scoolyz.
  • Block. You can block a person: you no longer see their comments, posts or chat messages, and they can't reply to your posts. They are not told. Unblock them at any time in Blocked people. School notices, homework and marks always reach you, and staff don't block people (they report them or use the Moderation queue).
  • Disagree with a decision? Talk to your school: the decision is the school's.
  • Serious cases. If something puts a child or anyone else at risk, the school may tell parents or the authorities. If you have reported something serious and the school does not act, write to us at haidarmortada30@gmail.com.

7. Suspending or closing access

  • Your school can pause your posting, or pause or close your account, under its own rules.
  • We can suspend or end access for a person or a school that seriously or repeatedly breaks these terms, puts others at risk or harms Scoolyz, or when the law requires it. We tell you why when we are allowed to.
  • If your school stops using Scoolyz, the accounts it created close; the school decides what happens to its records.

8. Deleting your account

  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or at scoolyz.com/delete-account if you can't use the app.
  • Your sign-in, photo, devices, notifications and Ask Scoolyz conversations are deleted at once. The school keeps its school records, including messages exchanged within the school, as the deletion page explains; ask your school if you want any of them erased.
  • A student's account closes once a parent confirms it. A parent can also close their children's sign-ins when closing their own account.

9. The service

  • We work to keep Scoolyz running, safe and correct, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes. We may change, add or remove features, and an old version of the app may have to be updated before it works again.
  • The bus position on the map can be late or wrong (for example when the driver's phone loses signal). Don't rely on it alone for a child's safety.
  • Ask Scoolyz, the built-in assistant, can be wrong. Check anything important with your school.
