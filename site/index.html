Your school, in one app

Scoolyz is the school management system your school runs. Parents, students and staff use the same app to follow attendance, grades and report cards, fees, homework, the school bus, the timetable and the school's notices, in English, French or Arabic.

Get it on Google Play

Open the app, then search for your school by name or enter the code your school gave you. Your school creates your account; ask it for your sign-in details.

Parents

Your children's attendance, marks, report cards, homework and fees, the bus on a map, and the school's notices.

Staff

Take attendance, enter marks, publish homework and report cards, and reach families, from a phone or a computer.

Schools

Each school has its own Scoolyz, at its own address on scoolyz.com. Its records stay the school's.
