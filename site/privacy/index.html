Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Scoolyz is a school management system. Each school runs its own Scoolyz site at <school>.scoolyz.com, and parents, students and staff use the Scoolyz app (Android, Windows) or a web browser to reach it. This policy explains what is collected, why, who sees it and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible

  • Your school decides what it records about its students, families and staff, and why. It is responsible for those school records (it is the "controller"). Questions about a school record go to your school first.
  • Scoolyz provides the software and runs it for the school (it is the school's "processor"), and is responsible for the app itself: the school directory and push notifications. Contact: rabihmtd@gmail.com.

2. Who has an account

Your school creates every account: staff, parents and guardians, and students (students usually get their own sign-in). Nobody can sign up in the app on their own.

Schools may also record live lessons in class rooms, including the voices of those taking part. Recordings are deleted 90 days after the end of their school year at the latest, sooner if the school chooses. (The Android app does not use the microphone.)

4. What the app asks your phone for

  • Camera: to scan your school's QR code and to take a photo you choose to send (for example a doctor's note). Only when you tap it.
  • Location: only for school bus drivers during a trip, and for the school map if you open it. A trip shows a notification while the position is shared.
  • Notifications: to tell you about notices, marks, homework, fees and the bus.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on the app lock. The app never receives your fingerprint or face; Android checks it and only answers "yes".

5. Who it is shared with

Scoolyz does not sell personal data, shows no advertising, and contains no analytics or tracking tools. Within your school, people see what their role allows (a parent sees their own children; a teacher their classes). Outside the school, data goes only to services needed to run Scoolyz:

  • the company hosting the school's server, and an encrypted backup store;
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging, which delivers notifications to Android phones (it receives the phone's token, its Firebase installation ID and the notification's text);
  • the email provider your school uses, for emails the school sends;
  • map data from OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap, which your school's server fetches and keeps a copy of. Your device asks only your school's server for maps, so these services never receive your device's IP address (they see the school server's);
  • OpenStreetMap's place search (Nominatim), which receives, from the school's server, the place names staff type to find a bus stop or the school, never anyone's position;
  • if your school turns it on, the learning platform Archimind, when an adult (or a student in a web browser) chooses to sign in to it from Scoolyz (name, email and role). Students never see it in the Android app.

We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

6. How long it is kept

Your school decides how long it keeps its records. Some things are removed automatically: Ask Scoolyz conversations and live-lesson recordings 90 days after the end of their school year (your school can set a different period), read notifications after 90 days, bus positions after 90 days, server logs after 14 days, backups after at most 8 weeks.

7. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored as secure hashes. Backups are encrypted. Access inside the school follows each person's role. The app only opens your school's own address on scoolyz.com; other links open in your browser.

8. Children

Students, including children under 13, use Scoolyz with accounts their school creates. Nobody can sign up on their own.

  • The school decides. The school is responsible for the student data it enters and for informing parents and obtaining any consent the law requires (for example under COPPA in the United States or the GDPR in the European Union). Scoolyz processes children's data only on the school's behalf and only to run the school.
  • What a student's account holds: their name, class, timetable, homework, marks and report cards, attendance, the school's notices, and what they hand in or write in the app (homework files, comments in their class, questions to Ask Scoolyz). Health and fee records are entered and seen by school staff and the student's parents, not by other students.
  • What we never do with children's data: no advertising, no profiling, no selling, no analytics or tracking tools, and no advertising ID or device identifiers (such as IMEI or MAC address).
  • Location: the app never collects the location of students or parents. Only a bus driver's phone shares its position, and only during a trip.
  • Who students can talk to: only their school: their teachers and class, in class spaces the school controls. Students' comments, class posts and live-lesson chat are off unless the school turns them on. The first time a student posts, they are reminded how to stay safe, and a message with a phone number, email, address or social media account waits for a teacher's approval.
  • Parents' rights: a parent sees their child's school information in their own account and can ask the school to correct it. A student who asks to close their account must wait for a parent to confirm it on their own profile. A parent can also close their children's sign-ins when closing their own account, or at scoolyz.com/delete-account. The school keeps the records the law requires it to keep.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete my account) or at scoolyz.com/delete-account. Your sign-in, photo, devices and notifications are deleted at once; the school keeps its records, as explained there. A student's account closes once a parent confirms.
  • Ask your school to see, correct or erase records about you or your child, as the law allows.
  • Turn notifications, the camera or location off at any time in your phone's settings.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date. Important changes will be announced in the app.
